🔬 What it is

A small round blue-cell sarcoma of bone and soft tissue, part of the Ewing sarcoma family of tumors (ESFT). Driven by a fusion oncoprotein — most often EWSR1-FLI1 from t(11;22)(q24;q12) — which acts as an aberrant transcription factor.

📊 Epidemiology

  • 2nd most common bone cancer in children/young adults.
  • ~200 new US cases/year; slight male predominance; more common in those of European ancestry.
  • Peak in adolescence; can occur at any age.

📍 Presentation & sites

  • Localized pain & swelling, sometimes with fever — can mimic infection.
  • Common sites: pelvis, femur, chest wall (Askin tumor), other long bones; extraskeletal forms occur.
  • Pathologic fracture or a palpable mass may be the first sign.

🧪 Diagnosis & staging

  • Biopsy with IHC (membranous CD99) + molecular confirmation of EWSR1 rearrangement (FISH / RT-PCR).
  • Staging workup: MRI of primary, chest CT, FDG-PET and/or bone scan, bone-marrow assessment.
  • Key risk drivers: presence of metastases, tumor size/volume, primary site (axial/pelvic worse), response to induction.

💉 Detailed Chemotherapy

First-line backbone, dosing, the alternating schedule, interval compression, and relapse options — with sources.

1 · First-line backbone: VDC alternating with IE

Five-drug program given as two alternating cycle types, 14 cycles total (≈6–9 months), now standardly interval-compressed every 2 weeks with G-CSF support. Standard of care across COG (AEWS0031) and EURO-EWING.

2 · Interval compression level 1

Giving VDC/IE every 2 weeks vs every 3 weeks (with G-CSF) improved outcomes without more toxicity:

📈 5-year EFS 73% (q2-week) vs 65% (q3-week) — Womer et al., COG AEWS0031, J Clin Oncol 2012.

Local control (surgery and/or radiotherapy) is integrated with chemotherapy after induction.

3 · 2024 trial update upfront targeted

INTER-EWING-1 (Cancer Research UK, Sep 2024) — the largest all-ages Ewing trial, three randomizations:

  • Maintenance chemotherapy
  • Radiotherapy dose-finding
  • Regorafenib first-line for metastatic disease — the first targeted drug studied upfront in Ewing sarcoma.
Cancer Research UK ↗

4 · Recurrent / refractory no standard

No single standardized 2nd-line regimen; commonly used combinations:

  • Topotecan + cyclophosphamide
  • Irinotecan + temozolomide (± vincristine at some centers)
  • Gemcitabine + docetaxel
  • High-dose chemo + autologous stem-cell rescue in selected high-risk cases

5 · Relapsed disease consensus (2025)

  • Local & metastasis-directed therapy (LMDT) individualized in a multidisciplinary setting — curative-intent or palliative depending on burden & prior therapy.
  • 5-year survival for metastatic relapsed/refractory ES remains ~10–20%.
Shah et al., Cancer 2025 (PMID 40251761) ↗
